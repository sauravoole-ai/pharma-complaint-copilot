Subject: Discoloration observed in Amoxicillin 500 mg capsules

Northstar Pharmacy contacted us by email on 10 September 2026. The customer reported that 48 capsules of Amoxicillin 500 mg from batch AMX240602 appeared darker than usual when the bottle was opened. No patient adverse event was reported. The bottle has been set aside and will not be dispensed.

The customer requested replacement stock and a quality investigation into the affected batch.
